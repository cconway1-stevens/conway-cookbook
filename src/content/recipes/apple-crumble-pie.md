## Ingredients

### For the crumble

- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 cup old-fashioned rolled oats (not quick cooking)
- ⅓ cup packed light brown sugar (65 g)
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- ½ teaspoon kosher salt
- 10 tablespoons unsalted butter, chilled and cut into ½-inch pieces

### For the pie

- 1 recipe chilled homemade pie dough (enough for one crust)
- All-purpose flour, for rolling
- 3 pounds Granny Smith apples, peeled, cored, and cut into ½-inch wedges
- ½ cup packed light brown sugar
- 3 tablespoons cornstarch
- 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- 2 teaspoons apple cider vinegar
- ½ teaspoon kosher salt
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, chilled and cut into ½-inch pieces

## Directions

1. **Make the crumble.** Mix the flour, oats, sugar, cinnamon, and salt. Rub in the butter with your fingertips until no dry flour remains and the mixture clumps when squeezed. Cover and refrigerate.
2. **Heat the oven.** Place a rack in the lower third and preheat to 425°F. Line a rimmed baking sheet with foil or a silicone mat.
3. **Roll out the crust.** Let the dough soften for a minute or two, then roll it on a floured surface into a 13-inch circle about ⅛ inch thick.
4. **Line the pie dish.** Press the dough into the base and sides of the dish. Trim to a ½-inch overhang, fold it under, and crimp the edge. Freeze the crust uncovered while you mix the filling.
5. **Mix the filling.** Toss the apple wedges with brown sugar, cornstarch, cinnamon, vinegar, and salt until evenly coated.
6. **Fill the shell.** Fit about a quarter of the apples snugly across the bottom. Mound the rest in the center with any juices from the bowl, pressing gently to compact them. Dot with the butter, then scatter the chilled crumble over the top, breaking up large clumps.
7. **Bake and cool.** Set the pie dish on the lined sheet and bake for 15 minutes. Lower the oven to 350°F and bake another 60–75 minutes, until the crust is golden and the filling bubbles. If the topping darkens early, loosely tent the center with foil. Cool completely before slicing.

## Make ahead

Store the cooled pie loosely covered at room temperature for up to four days. The crust is crispest on the first two days.
