## Ingredients

- 1½ cups all-purpose flour
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1½ teaspoons baking powder
- ½ teaspoon baking soda
- ½ teaspoon fine salt
- 1¼ cups buttermilk
- 1 large egg
- 3 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted and cooled, plus more for the skillet
- Maple syrup, for serving

## Directions

1. Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a large bowl.
2. In another bowl, whisk the buttermilk and egg, then whisk in the melted butter. Fold the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients just until no pockets of flour remain. Let the batter sit for 5 minutes.
3. Heat a skillet over medium heat and brush with butter. Ladle in ¼ cup batter per pancake. Cook until bubbles open on top and the edges look set, about 2 minutes. Flip and cook until golden, 1 to 2 minutes more.
4. Serve warm with butter and maple syrup.
