## Ingredients

- 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- ½ teaspoon black pepper
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 3 garlic cloves, lightly crushed
- ½ cup low-sodium chicken broth
- 1 lemon, half juiced and half thinly sliced
- 4 sprigs fresh thyme

## Directions

1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Pat the chicken dry and season all over with salt and pepper.
2. Heat the oil in an oven-safe skillet over medium heat. Add the thighs skin-side down and cook until the skin is deep golden, about 8–10 minutes. Flip the thighs.
3. Add the garlic, broth, lemon juice, and thyme around the thighs, then tuck the lemon slices into the pan.
4. Roast until the thickest part of each thigh reaches at least 165°F without touching the bone, about 20–25 minutes. Rest for 5 minutes, then spoon the pan juices around the chicken.
